# Sharing deck Storyline policy

## 1. Audience outcome

Use this policy when the primary goal is to create understanding, alignment, or discussion by communicating experience, insights, progress, practices, or a point of view.

Define what the audience should understand differently, why the topic matters now, and whether the intended ending is reflection, alignment, discussion, or action.

## 2. Storyline logic

Build the audience journey through:

`why it matters → relevant context or experience → key ideas and evidence → implications for the audience → discussion or proportionate action`

Use examples, evidence, and experience to make the ideas concrete. Organize around a small number of meaningful messages rather than a catalogue of topics.

## 3. Safeguards

- Do not manufacture a sales thesis, proposed scope, or commercial decision journey.
- Do not add exercises, knowledge checks, or learning objectives unless a confirmed Training behavior materially changes the Storyline.
- Label opinions, hypotheses, and illustrative examples; do not present them as established evidence.
- Do not claim that sharing information alone will resolve issues driven by policy, incentives, ownership, technology, data, or adoption.

## 4. Completion checks

Confirm that each page advances the audience's understanding, the evidence supports the stated point of view, the implications follow from the material presented, and the ending matches the intended discussion or action.
